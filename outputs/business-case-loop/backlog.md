# Validation backlog

Updated 2026-10-31. Initial cycle; no customer or model-performance validation completed.

## P0 — Prove the changing-blocker workflow
Owner: implementation and reactive-fit agents, lead synthesizes.
Create a temporal replay of one account with at least five events: initial blocker, seller reassurance without buyer confirmation, buyer confirmation, contradictory new requirement, irrelevant update. Include a synthetic case with unambiguous expected states and a separately annotated real/supplied dataset case. Do not label synthetic data as customer evidence.
Acceptance: every state change names its source, time and actor; seller reassurance alone never resolves a buyer blocker; old reports remain inspectable; unrelated events do not change the account state. Document current behavior and missing features separately. Confirm account scoping before pitching a per-account feature.

## P0 — Separate eventing value from Reactor memory value
Owner: reactive-fit agent.
Compare manual refresh, event-triggered stateless RAG, persistent Reactor history and full-context recomputation on identical event streams and evidence budgets. Pre-register outcome correctness, unsupported resolutions, stale state, relevant-change recall, source validity, latency and cost. Offline fixture/experiment design is authorized; paid model execution requires separate authorization.
Acceptance: choose a baseline, record actual measurements when available, and reject claims of Reactor advantage if it does not outperform the baseline on a user-relevant dimension. Eventing alone is not evidence for persistent-memory superiority.

## P1 — Validate actual buyer demand
Owner: market agent.
Prepare a short interview protocol for sales managers/account executives: last missed blocker, consequence, current workaround, frequency, willingness to change workflow and pay. Find evidence of actual workflow needs through primary sources; distinguish vendor positioning from user validation. Do not contact people automatically.
Acceptance: explicit target buyer and painful decision, current workaround, measurable outcome and evidence that could disqualify the use case. Market research alone does not mark this task validated.

## P1 — Challenge differentiation
Owner: market agent with reactive-fit critique.
Compare the precise proposed workflow against current Gong and CRM warnings and adjacent feedback tools. Distinguish warning clearance semantics from blocker-resolution semantics. Do not infer lack of competitor capability from an omitted documentation feature.
Acceptance: source-backed comparison plus a narrow, testable differentiator; withdraw differentiation if feature parity or lack of user value is demonstrated.
