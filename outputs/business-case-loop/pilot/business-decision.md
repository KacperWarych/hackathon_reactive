# Business decision — immediate research cycle, 31 October 2026

## Recommendation

Test a **human-reviewed client change decision service for fixed-scope agencies** before building a standalone SaaS product. Sell preparation of an actionable, sourced decision and maintenance of its reviewed status. Treat the existing Reactive AI application as a possible delivery aid, not the buyer's reason to purchase.

Agency scope review is the first experiment because a bounded text packet can produce first value without connectors and the delivery lead may also control spending. These are testable assumptions. Support handoff remains the strongest alternative if a committed team and representative cases become available. There is no validated business winner.

## Concrete offer hypothesis

Two weeks, one project, up to five change episodes, next-agreed-business-day delivery, source-backed briefs, one correction pass and a maintained reviewed ledger. Proposed price: PLN 750 before applicable taxes. This is an experimental asking price, not researched willingness to pay. See offer-one-page.md for the reviewable draft and the detailed operating analysis in ../2026-10-31/paid-pilot-offer.md.

## Buyer-value check: do not confuse our margin with their benefit

At the proposed PLN 750 fee, time savings alone require 3.75 net hours if the buyer values time at PLN 200/hour. Both the hourly rate and scenario are illustrative assumptions, not observed economics. Across five episodes that is 45 net minutes saved per episode. If normal preparation is only 15 minutes, time savings alone cannot justify this fee even if our own operation is profitable. At PLN 100/hour the requirement rises to 7.5 net hours; at PLN 300/hour it falls to 2.5 hours.

Measure source collection, review and correction as part of customer effort. Do not add guessed avoided rework or hypothetical extra billing to make the model look positive. Record a separate verified consequence only when the buyer supplies evidence, and distinguish causation from coincidence. If net benefit is weak, reduce the offer, find a more costly job, or reject it rather than asserting premium AI value.

## Strongest disconfirming evidence

Public practitioner anecdotes describe shared change logs, deliberate concessions and an existing custom Monday-plus-AI scope comparison. They are unverified but directly challenge novelty and the assumption that detection solves the pain. Our interviews must distinguish missed changes, evidence reconstruction, unrecorded approvals and deliberate commercial concessions. Vendor tools already offer broader agency operations, contextual support assistance and sales briefs; generic summarization is not a sufficient paid wedge.

## Current readiness and limits

Created now: market comparison, operating offer and costs, skeptic review, one-page offer, synthetic example deliverable, interview script and empty evidence register. Existing application reuse is versioned cited report revision; it does not provide reliable project/actor authority or an approved-baseline ledger. Those controls would be maintained and checked manually in the proposed pilot. A source quote is not proof of approval.

No customer was contacted, no money was accepted, no API benchmark was run and no application feature was implemented in this research cycle. All pilot results remain unknown. Synthetic examples specify expected behavior and are not evidence of demand or performance.

## Next evidence that can change the decision

Run five qualified problem interviews using recent source-backed cases. Continue only if at least three demonstrate repeated preparation/recordkeeping pain and at least two supply usable material with a reviewer. These are proposed screening gates, not market estimates. Compare assisted output with the buyer's actual process and a shared-log/checklist baseline. Seek an explicitly paid, bounded engagement; after delivery, seek paid continuation and record why it succeeds or fails.

If agency demand fails those gates, test support handoffs only with a real willing partner and the incumbent as baseline. Avoid another round of generic vendor browsing when the missing evidence is a buyer, a permissioned case and a purchasing decision.
