# Client change review — proposed paid pilot

**Draft offer for review; not sent, sold or activated.**

For an agency owner or delivery lead who spends time reconstructing what was agreed before responding to client changes.

Over two weeks, we review up to five potential changes on one project and prepare a brief showing:

- What the accepted brief says, with source references.
- What the new request changes or leaves unclear.
- What is requested versus actually approved.
- What needs an estimate, clarification or decision from your team.
- A draft clarification for you to review, plus an updated change log.

You provide the approved baseline, relevant messages and a named reviewer. Each episode is capped at five relevant source items or 10,000 words, whichever limit is reached first. One correction pass is included. Delivery is by the next agreed business day after receiving a complete packet.

**Proposed pilot fee: PLN 750 before applicable taxes.** This is a price hypothesis for testing, not an established market rate. The service is manually reviewed. It does not promise recovered revenue or decide whether work is legally chargeable.

We begin with a 45-minute project setup and close by comparing preparation/review time and useful decisions against your current process. You retain the final say on scope, pricing, schedule and all client communications. If the accepted baseline or approval authority cannot be established, we report that uncertainty.

No inbox integration or subscription commitment is required for the proposed pilot. Source handling, access, retention and the delivery window must be agreed before any live engagement. No pilot has begun.
